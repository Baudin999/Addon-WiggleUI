# The WiggleUI guide

An interface for TBC Anniversary (2.5.6) and Classic Era (1.15.9). It draws your
unit frames, your action bars, your bags, your character sheet, your quest log,
your map, your mail and most of the other windows the client has, and about a
dozen readouts neither client has ever had.

This is a guide rather than a feature list. It starts at the install, goes
through the five questions the addon asks the first time you log in, and then
walks the screen in the order you meet it. Every section names the slash command
for what it just described, because everything in the settings window has one.

## The screen, in the order you meet it

- [Fighting](fighting.md). The Charge button, raid marking, casting on what the
  mouse is over, the swing timer and the Slam band, the nag row, cooldowns and
  the totem bar.
- [Action bars](bars.md). Your own bars on your own slots, and the rings.
- [Frames](frames.md). You, your target, the party, the raid and the enemy bars.
- [Windows](windows.md). The character sheet, sockets, dungeon log, quest log,
  map, talents, spell book, bags, merchant, mail and chat.
- [Feeds and meters](feeds.md). Loot and combat, damage and threat, and the
  breakdown of what your character actually does.
- [Chores](chores.md). Looting, selling, repairs and the rest of what it does
  for you.
- [The screen itself](screen.md). The minimap, the experience rails, tooltips
  and the Edit Mode layout.

## This is a beta

Everything here is built, has a switch of its own and gets played every day.
What it has not had is a wide test. Install it to help find what is broken
rather than an hour before a raid.

Bugs and requests go to
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/Baudin999/Addon-WiggleUI/issues), not to the
comments on the CurseForge page. Comments there are not tracked and get lost
between file uploads.
