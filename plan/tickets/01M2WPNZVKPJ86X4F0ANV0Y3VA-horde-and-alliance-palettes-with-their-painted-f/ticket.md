art/horde.jpeg and art/alliance.jpeg are two new palettes. Both paintings are
arcane's frame and floor repainted: Orgrimmar clay with the horde crest, and
Stormwind navy with the alliance lion.

Bake: arcane's inset, corner, floor tile (381, 313, 333 x 150) and clear 105.
The side rail is 150 long, not 220, because the pillar is a plain slab between
blocks at 200 and 490.

Margins: horde's dark brown goes with the blurred test. Alliance's navy does
not: the bottom rail's smooth face is low detail and joins the margin, and the
test ate it. So it uses the flat flood. The old white flood already compares
to the corner's colour, so it is renamed "flat" and not duplicated.

Palette files Theme/Horde.lua and Theme/Alliance.lua, on Theme.PALETTES and in
both TOCs.
