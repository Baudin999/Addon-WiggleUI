art/arcane.jpeg is a new palette: an indigo floor of carved star-slabs in the
desert's frame, re-skinned silver and gold.

Two ways it differs from the first two paintings, both handled in the bake:
- no white margin; the frame sits on a blurred nebula. The nebula is out of
  focus and the frame is not, so the margin is the low-detail region
  connected to the image's edge.
- the floor does not repeat down: three different rows of slabs, the bottom
  one of one-off pieces. The tile is the middle row, mortar line to mortar
  line (313 to 463) and one slab period across (333).

Palette file Theme/Arcane.lua, on Theme.PALETTES and in both TOCs.
