title: "Each mode ships its own screen, and the setup lays it on the profile"
---

Shipped.lua carries a common screen plus one table per mode (informational,
immersive, exploration), baked from three profiles of those names on the
author's account. Nothing is pushed into a player's profile: finishing the
setup lays the chosen mode's screen over the worn profile, and that is how a
player takes a screen shipped after theirs was made. The reset lands on the
screen of the mode the profile is in and keeps the mode.
