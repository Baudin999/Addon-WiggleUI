A first-login setup, four questions on cards with a picture of each answer, asked again from the game menu (WarriorKit Setup) and /wk setup:

- mode: immersive, exploration, informational (ns.db.theme)
- colours: the six palettes, each on its own painting
- unit frames: modern (portraits off) or flat (portraits on)
- tooltips: every type bottom right, or every type attached

Everything else ships as Core/Shipped.lua has it. The four answers never go into the bake: Setup.Asks names them and bake-defaults.lua steps over them.
