title: "The ring wears the theme, and the slice you are over is the one fired"

The ring under a bar's key was a flat disc in the window colour with the
squares laid on it. Two things wrong with that.

It is not the theme. Every window in the addon is the palette's painting with
a rail round it, and the one part of the addon you look at while your thumb is
on a key was the one part drawn in a colour of its own.

And it does not show the gesture. A push picks the slice it points into, not
the square it lands on, so the slice is the thing being aimed at and the ring
drew no slices. There is no picture of where one stops and the next begins,
and no picture of the middle, which is the part of the circle a release fires
nothing from.

Draw the pie: the palette's paper cut round, a seam on every boundary, the lit
slice under the cursor, and the hole at the radius the reach is measured from.
One drawing, used by the ring on the screen and by the circle on the settings
page.
