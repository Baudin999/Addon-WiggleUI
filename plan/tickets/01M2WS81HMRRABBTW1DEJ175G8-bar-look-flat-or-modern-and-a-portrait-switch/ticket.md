Two settings on the Theme page, beside the palette, drawn at the next reload like it:

- bar look, a dropdown: flat (today) or modern;
- portraits, a check.

Modern is three things, from comparing our frames with retail's and with ElvUI/BenikUI screenshots:
a vertical sheen on every gauge fill, health stacked tight on power with no hairline between,
and one outer edge per unit in the palette's accent instead of a dimmed copy of the fill.
The enemy bars take the sheen only; their edge carries reaction and stays.

Portraits off keeps the block its width: the gauge takes the square's room.
