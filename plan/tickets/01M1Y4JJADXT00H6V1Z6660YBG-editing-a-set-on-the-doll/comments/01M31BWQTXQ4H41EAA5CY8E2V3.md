Shipped as something else, 2026-09-21. Dropping this.

Phase 3 wanted an edit mode: pick a set, the nineteen rows draw what the set holds instead of what you have on, and the figure wears the set. That is not what got built. 7eadd84f and 76822054 put a row of small circles under every gear row instead, one per set, and every gesture this card asked for landed on them.

A drop from a bag fills the slot. A click takes what you are wearing into it. A drag off unsets it. A right click cycles through the deliberate hole, which is the state this card called shift right click. So the three slot states are visible and reachable, which is what the card says it exists for.

The circles beat the edit mode on the one thing that decided it: no mode. The disc stays what you are wearing, which is the sheet's job, and the set sits under it, so you read the set against your body rather than instead of it. There is no tint or edge to explain because the page never lies about what you have on.

What this card asked for and the circles do not give: building a set out of pieces you are not carrying, from a body you cannot see. That is worth having one day and it is not this card, it is a picker, which this card argues against for reasons that still hold.
