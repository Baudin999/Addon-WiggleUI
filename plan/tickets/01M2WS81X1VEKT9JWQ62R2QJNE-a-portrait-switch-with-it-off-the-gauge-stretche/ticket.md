`ns.db.portraits`, default on, drawn at reload. `Block.Place` drops the square and its divider from the
Flow row and keeps `total`, so the gauge column's grow takes the width. The name and level anchor to the
block's edge, the badges to the block's outer corners (the same pixels the square's were), and the ammo
pill docks under the block's portrait-side corner at the square's old width.
