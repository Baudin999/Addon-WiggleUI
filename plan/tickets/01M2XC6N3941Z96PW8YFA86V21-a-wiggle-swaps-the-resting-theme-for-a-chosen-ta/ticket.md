title: A wiggle swaps the resting theme for a chosen target theme and back
---

Replaces the exploration-only pin. Two settings: the theme at rest, and per theme a wiggle target (a theme or none). Defaults: exploration -> informational, others none. A wiggle redresses every worn frame with the target's modes; the next puts the rest back. The wiggled state is saved and survives /reload. Every element whose mode differs between rest and target is veiled at login so a wiggle works in combat. The XP rail's style becomes per theme (exploration minimal) rather than a pin special case. Exploration keeps loot feed and meters hidden at rest; the target brings them.
