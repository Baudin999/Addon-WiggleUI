Done, check.sh at 0/0. The art is yours, not Dialogue UI's and not a painted
one: art/parchment.jpeg landed in the tree mid-task at the canonical 1408x768
with a flat white margin, so it went straight through bake-backdrops.sh. It
was named dandd.jpeg; renamed to match its palette, as every other painting in
art/ is.

Ruled out first. Copying Art/Book/TextureKit-Parchment.png out of the installed
DialogueUI: Peterodox's, no licence, and this repository is public, which is
the rule check.sh already keeps for Media/BestAround.mp3. A procedurally
painted sheet was built and thrown away once the real one arrived; if it is
ever wanted again it is in this task's history, not on disk.

Four things the bake could not already do, all of them because this is the
first painting whose frame and floor are the same material.

- ground "self" and flatten(). No second picture and no repeat to cut a tile
  at. The floor inside the inset is stretched to the canvas and levelled to
  the mean of the 40 pixel ring just inside the inset, which is the brightness
  the rails hand off at. Unlevelled it is the middle of the sheet, the
  lightest part, and every rail fades out onto a floor paler than itself: a
  bright rectangle round the inside of every window.
- lit, the whole painting at a fraction of itself, against darken which only
  takes the floor down. At darken the frame stayed bright over a dimmed ground
  and read as two sheets laid on each other. 0.46 is not taste: UI.Quality[1]
  is white and [2] is the hard green, neither is the palette's to repaint, and
  the sheet at full brightness puts a white item name on it at 1.4 to 1. Lit,
  the ground is 0.080 mean luminance, white reads at 8 to 1, the green at 6 and
  the quest orange at 3.4.
- fuzz per painting. This margin ramps out of a drop shadow over about fifty
  pixels and FUZZ's thirty left half of it, which is a grey halo round the
  sheet against the world.
- corner 150, not the 210 the carved frames take. A corner is opaque
  everywhere the rails do not cover it and fades only over FADE at its inner
  edge, so it stands on the floor for corner less inset. At 210 that was 30
  window units of sheet a shade off the ground under it, sitting in all four
  corners of every window. Measured at 120 first and 150 is with the folds in.

Rails repeat at 600 by 340 rather than a measured period. There is nothing to
be in phase with on a sheet; the only thing a join can show is the torn edge
stepping, and a longer tile is fewer joins out of the same size textures.

The other six palettes' textures are byte-identical after the rebake.

Not done: nothing has been looked at in the client yet. The geometry was
checked by compositing the nine baked pieces the way UI/Backdrop.lua lays them
out, at 420 by 300 and 300 by 200 units.
