A second style for the experience rails, `progressStyle = "minimal"`, beside
today's, which is named "expressive" and stays the default.

Minimal is DialogueUI's bar in this addon's language: the whole width of the
screen, flush with the bottom edge, 4 design pixels tall, no text, nine
one-pixel marks for ten blocks, the unit frames' tight chrome edge instead of
the palette floor, and a soft glow on the fill's leading edge. The hover keeps
the full tooltip. A watched faction stacks as a second line sharing the edge.
It is not draggable, because its place is the screen edge.

Picked in the panel with a Cycle and with `/wk xp style minimal|expressive`.
