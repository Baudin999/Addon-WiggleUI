Done. UI.SideTabs in UI/Window.lua beside UI.TabStrip: pooled, keyed by zone name, labels turned -90 degrees through FontString:SetRotation, probed and pcalled with upright labels as the fallback.

Watch for. A tab is as long as its own label and a zone is called Eastern Plaguelands, so ten tabs is 1300 pixels of strip on a 900 pixel panel. Side:Resize takes a longest and clips the label; Quests/Column.lua divides four fifths of the screen by how many tabs there are, with a floor of 54 units.

The choice is edge-triggered. stoodIn is written on every paint including to nil, which is what makes walking through a city work: Westfall to Stormwind records nothing and keeps the tab you pressed, and Stormwind back out to Westfall is a change from nothing to Westfall and takes it back. An earlier version only wrote non-nil and walking back into the zone you started in never dropped the choice.

Also. The column no longer hides itself when the zone under your feet has nothing in it, because that took the tabs away with the rows. 85-quest-column carries the strip's own block and the two city assertions were turned round.
