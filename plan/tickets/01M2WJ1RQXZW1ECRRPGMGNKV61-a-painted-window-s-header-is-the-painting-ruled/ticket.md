title: "A painted window's header is the painting, ruled like its footer"
---

In the client the bag window's footer read well over the painting and the
header did not: the title strip is a slab of flat chrome colour. On a window
with a backdrop, drop the strip and draw one hairline rule under the title
row at the footer's inset instead.
